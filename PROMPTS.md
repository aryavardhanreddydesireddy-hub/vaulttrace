# AI-assisted development log

This file must be updated while development happens. Do not invent dates, commits, bugs, or tests. Replace the example structure below with your real interactions and actual commit links.

## Entry template

### YYYY-MM-DD — Short task name
- **Tool:** ChatGPT / Claude / Copilot / other
- **What I asked:** Describe the exact prompt or link to the conversation.
- **What it produced:** Summarize the code or approach suggested.
- **What was wrong or incomplete:** Identify a specific issue you personally checked.
- **What I changed:** Explain your correction and why it is safer or more correct.
- **Verification:** Give the actual test/manual steps and result.
- **Commit:** Paste the real commit URL after committing.

## Required focus areas

Include at least five genuine dated entries over your work. At least two must cover authorization, access control, or audit logging. One possible review question is: "Does the download route verify the current user's exact document permission on every request, including expiry and membership status?" Another is: "Can the audit verification detect a changed event, and what attacks can still bypass this hash chain?"

Do not copy these prompts as completed entries. Record what you actually asked, observed, fixed, tested, and committed.
